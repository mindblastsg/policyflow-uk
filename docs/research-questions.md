# Research questions

PolicyFlow should be evaluated as research infrastructure, not simply as a software product.

## Core feasibility

1. Can public UK policy/regulatory processes be reconstructed as event flows with enough fidelity for serious research?
2. Which relationships can be extracted deterministically, and which require human or probabilistic judgement?
3. How often do official sources change without producing a genuinely material policy event?
4. How reliably can one policy process be matched across HMT, Parliament, legislation.gov.uk, FCA and PRA sources?
5. What is the smallest ontology that captures real policy branching without becoming a legal taxonomy project?

## Temporal research

6. How long do different categories of financial policy spend in each state?
7. Can policy velocity, acceleration, delay and withdrawal be measured reproducibly?
8. Which observable signals precede implementation, and how informative are they historically?
9. How frequently do proposals materially narrow or widen between consultation and final implementation?

## Institutional research

10. Where in the policy pipeline do material changes most often occur?
11. Which types of consultation feedback are associated with subsequent changes?
12. How do primary legislation, secondary legislation and regulator rules interact in financial-services policy flows?
13. Which areas of the system generate the greatest amendment and cross-reference complexity?

## Economic and market research

14. Can legal/regulatory events be mapped reproducibly to regulated activities, business models and listed firms?
15. Can a transparent regulatory-materiality score distinguish legally significant changes from financially significant changes?
16. Can firm-level regulatory exposure be represented as a measurable factor rather than narrative analyst judgement?
17. Do high-materiality policy events produce statistically identifiable changes in prices, volumes, volatility, credit spreads or analyst expectations?
18. How much information is contained in the *delta* between policy stages rather than in the final rule itself?

## Evaluation principle

A useful PolicyFlow variable must be reproducible from timestamped public evidence. If a score cannot be explained, reconstructed and challenged, it should not be treated as research-grade output.
